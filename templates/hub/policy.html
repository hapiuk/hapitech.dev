Our promise: honest reviews

HapiTech Reviews brings a local business's reviews together from across platforms (Google, Facebook and its own customers) so you can see the whole picture in one place. Reviews are only useful if they're honest, so here's exactly how it works.

Where reviews come from

  • Left here: customers can review a business directly. If we can match the reviewer to one of the business's jobs, it's marked "Verified customer".
  • From Google and Facebook: we bring in the business's reviews from those platforms and show each one, with the business's reply, as it appears there, labelled with where it came from and linked back to the original. Some dates are approximate, because those platforms only show "3 months ago" and so on. Facebook uses "recommends" rather than stars, so we show that instead.

We don't delete genuine reviews

Good or bad, a real review from a real customer stays up. Businesses can reply in public, but they can't edit, hide or remove reviews, and they can't pay to do so.

What we do remove

Only reviews that, after we've checked, turn out to be fake, for the wrong business, abusive or hateful, or that share someone's personal details. Reviews from Google or Facebook also follow those platforms' own rules.

Reporting a review

Every review has a Report link. Reports go straight to a person at HapiTech, not to the business. The review stays up while we look into it. If you leave your email, we'll tell you what we decided.

New reviews left here

To keep out spam, a review left by someone we can't match to the business's customers may wait for a quick check first. If nobody looks at it within 14 days, it goes live anyway.
