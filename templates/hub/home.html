How it works

One place for every review a business gets.

Reviews are scattered across Google, Facebook and more. We pull them together, keep them honest, and put them where customers will see them.

01 · COLLECT

From every platform

We bring in the business's reviews from Google and Facebook, plus reviews customers leave directly with HapiTech, each one labelled with where it came from.

G Googlef Facebook★ HapiTech
02 · KEEP IT HONEST

Nothing genuine hidden

Good or bad, real reviews stay. Businesses can reply in public, but they can't edit or delete reviews. Anyone can report a fake, and a person checks every report.

03 · SHOW IT OFF

Here and on their website

The same reviews appear on the business's own website automatically, with a page here that works even if they don't have a website at all.
