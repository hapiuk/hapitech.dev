How to export

  1. Google: open the business on Google Maps → Reviews tab → Sort: Newest.
  2. Facebook: open the page's Reviews tab (e.g. facebook.com/RayGHandymanservice/reviews), logged in.
  3. Press F12 → Console, paste the script below and press Enter. (Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first.)
  4. Leave the tab alone until it says Done; a .json file downloads. Upload it here.

The scripts only read the page, they don't post or change anything. Copied reviews keep the business's replies and link back to the original.
