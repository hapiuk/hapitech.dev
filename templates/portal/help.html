Help

Quick guides. Can't find it? Ask below and Aaron will help.

⭐ Get a review after a job
  1. Tap Ask at the bottom of the screen (or "Job done ✓" on a job).
  2. Show the customer the QR code. They scan it with their phone camera and tap the stars.
  3. Not with them? Type their email instead and we'll send the link.

Reviews appear on your website within 10 minutes.

💼 From enquiry to invoice
  1. Quote requests from your website appear in Jobs. Add phone enquiries with Add a job.
  2. Open the job and Write a quote. They get an email and can accept it with one tap.
  3. Book it in with a date. They get a confirmation, and a reminder the day before.
  4. Afterwards tap Job done ✓, then Send the invoice.
📅 See jobs in your phone's calendar

Open Calendar and follow "Add to your phone's calendar". It's set up once and updates by itself.

📱 Put this app on your home screen

iPhone: in Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: tap Install on the banner, or the browser menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen.

📸 Post your work

Go to Posts → New post. Add a title like "New bathroom in Tettenhall" and a few photos. Posts with the town name help local people find you on Google.

✏️ Change your website

Go to My website to change your service text and photos. Changes appear within 10 minutes, and "Undo" puts back the last version.

🏖️ Going on holiday

In Settings, set "Back on". Your website shows a notice until then and booking requests pause.

👷 Give a helper a login

In Settings → Add a helper. They log in with their own email and can't see money or settings.
