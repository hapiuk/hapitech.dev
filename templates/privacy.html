Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

This site is operated by Aaron Gomm, trading as HapiTech ("HapiTech", "we", "us"), a sole trader based in Oxford, UK.

Address: 2 Deer Walk, Oxford, OX4 7XX
Contact: aaron@hapitech.dev

We're the data controller for the personal data described below.

2. What data we collect

Contact form. When you use the "Get in Touch" form on this site, we collect the name, email address and message you provide. To help filter spam and respond appropriately, we also capture your IP address, browser user-agent, referring page and the time of submission. This is sent to us by email — it isn't stored in a database.

Client accounts. If you're a HapiTech client with dashboard access, we hold your name, email address and phone number so we can manage the relationship and give you access. There are no passwords: you log in with a 6-digit code we email you, which expires after 10 minutes and is never stored in readable form.

Customer reviews. We run a review system for the local businesses we work with. You can leave a review from the business's website, by scanning a QR code they show you, or from a link they email you. If they email you the link, your email address is used to send that one message and isn't stored. If you leave a review, we store your rating, what you wrote, the name you chose to show and any photos you add. Photos are resized and re-encoded, which removes hidden details such as the location your phone recorded. Your review is shown publicly on that business's website and review page under the name you entered.

Security records. To stop spam on review forms and repeated login attempts, we keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your IP address, and for logins the email address tried, for up to 7 days. We can't turn the hash back into your IP address.

Client posts and photos. Businesses using their HapiTech dashboard can post updates and photos of their work, which we store and show on their website.

3. Why we process this data

  • Contact form: legitimate interest — responding to enquiries you've sent us.
  • Client accounts: contract / legitimate interest — providing the service you've engaged us for.
  • Review requests and security records: legitimate interest. The business asks for feedback on work it did for you, and we keep the system free of spam and break-ins.
  • Reviews you post: consent. You choose to write and publish the review, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

4. Where your data is stored

Everything above is stored on our own server, a VPS hosted with Namecheap, Inc. Outgoing email (contact form notifications, login codes, review requests) also goes through Namecheap's email service. We don't use any other third party for this — no analytics, no ad networks, no data brokers — and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

5. Cookies

We only use one cookie: a signed session cookie that keeps you logged in to your dashboard, and protects forms (including the review form) against misuse. It's strictly necessary for the site to function and is deleted when you log out or it expires. We don't use analytics or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie consent banner.

6. How long we keep it

  • Contact form submissions exist only as an email in our inbox, kept as long as any other business correspondence, then deleted at our discretion.
  • Client account data is kept for the duration of the working relationship and a reasonable period afterward for accounting/legal purposes.
  • Reviews are kept while the business shows them. You can ask us to delete your review at any time.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you
  • correct inaccurate data
  • delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • give you a copy of your data in a portable format

To do any of this, email aaron@hapitech.dev. We'll respond within one month. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes. Significant changes will be reflected in the date above.
