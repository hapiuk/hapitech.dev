    🌌 Welcome! Quick Guide

    This is a real-time Solar System explorer. It’s built to be fun to use and grounded in real orbital data — but not perfectly to-scale (space is huge 😅).

    🕹 How to use it:

    • Left-click a planet, moon, or the Explorer Station to select it.
    • Right-drag to rotate/look around; Scroll to zoom.
    • W / A / S / D to fly the camera freely.
    • Focus to lock the camera onto the selected body.
    • Scan a body to collect data and earn Exploration Points (EP).
    • Use Time controls to jump forward/back and watch orbits move.

    ✅ What’s accurate (the science bit):

    • Planet paths are calculated using Kepler-style orbital elements.
    • Moon orbits use real data from NASA/JPL Horizons (J2000 epoch), orbiting their parent planet.
    • Rotation speeds follow the configured rotation hours (including retrograde spins).

    🎨 What’s visual-only (by design):

    • Planet sizes and moon distances are scaled for readability.
    • Some moons may look “too close” because a true scale would make them tiny dots.
    • Lighting, glow, and the starfield are stylised.

    Coming soon: journals + telescope image uploads tied to planets, moons (and later satellites too).
