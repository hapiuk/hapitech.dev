## Everything that's new: your website and your dashboard

Hi {name},

Here's the full list of what I've been working on for {business}. Your website looks the same as before, just tidier, but a lot has improved behind it, and you now have your own dashboard to run it all.

**Your reviews**

- A brand new reviews section on your website, with your overall score and a row of reviews customers can swipe through.
- Your **Google** and **Facebook** reviews come in automatically, alongside reviews left with you directly. Each one is clearly marked with where it came from, in that site's colours.
- Your own review page on **HapiTech Reviews** (hapitech.report), with all your reviews and ratings from every site in one place.
- Customers can leave a review in about 20 seconds from a QR code or link, and are then offered a quick way to post it on Google too.
- Spam protection: a review from someone we can't match to one of your jobs waits for your OK first.
- Reply to reviews from your phone. You can mark ones that don't need a reply, and your **reply rate** shows on your Home page.
- Search your reviews by words, name, stars or where they came from.

**Jobs and enquiries**

- Quote requests from your website land in **Jobs** automatically.
- Send a quote, book a date (your customer gets a reminder the day before), mark it done, then send the invoice.
- Shortcuts to the sites you find work on, like Bark, TaskRabbit and MyBuilder.
- A calendar you can add to your phone's own calendar.

**Your website**

- Change your wording, sections, dropdowns, photos, privacy policy and terms yourself, in **My website**.
- **Post your latest work:** add photos and a line about the job. It gets its own page on your website, goes into your gallery, and you can share it in one tap.
- Visitors can tap **♥** on photos they like, and you can put the most-liked ones at the top of your homepage.
- Opening a photo now fills the screen on phones, and you can swipe through them.
- **Away mode:** going on holiday? Your website tells customers when you're back.
- Better page titles and descriptions for Google, and a nicer preview when your site is shared on Facebook or WhatsApp.
- Your contact form quietly blocks spam bots, so you get far fewer junk enquiries.
- Visitor counts without cookies or tracking, so there's no cookie banner, and you can see how people find you.
- Your privacy policy is updated to match how everything works now.
- (Roland) The photo viewer no longer jumps about when you flick through photos, and the "See all our recent work" link is easier to read.
- (Ray) Your project photos are back at the top of the page and in your gallery, the big heading is calmer, there's less empty space between sections, and the menu works properly on phones.

**Your dashboard**

- Works on your phone like an app: add it to your home screen, and log in with a code by email (no password).
- In your own colours, with light mode if you prefer.
- **My plan:** your Friends plan, and a history of anything paid.
- **Request work:** ask me for changes or new features, and offer an amount if you like.
- **Extras:** add-ons I can switch on for you, like an AI chat assistant for your website (coming soon).
- Every page has a short "How does this work?" tip, and there's a Help page.

That's a lot, so take it at your own pace. Use as much or as little as you like, and just reply if you have any questions or ideas.
