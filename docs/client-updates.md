## A few improvements to your website

Hi {name},

I've been working on your website behind the scenes. It looks the same as before, but quite a lot has improved.

**Reviews**

- New reviews now appear on your website automatically.
- The **"Leave a Review"** button goes to your own review page, so reviews land straight on your site. Customers are then offered a quick way to post the same review on Google too.
- Each Google review now has a **"via Google"** link that opens your Google reviews, so people can see they're real.
- Spam protection: if someone we can't match to one of your jobs leaves a review, you get to check it before it goes live.
- (Ray) Your reviews now show the "via Google" link too, like the rest of your site.

**Photos**

- Opening a photo now fills the screen properly on phones, and you can swipe through them.
- Visitors can tap **♥** on photos they like. You can choose to show the most-liked ones at the top of your homepage.
- (Roland) The photo viewer had a habit of jumping about when you flicked through photos. That's fixed.
- (Ray) Your project photos are back on your website, at the top of the page and in your gallery.

**New: a "recent work" page**

- When you post photos of a finished job, it gets its own page on your website, and the photos go into your gallery too. Posts that mention the town help local people find you on Google.

**Behind the scenes**

- Your site now counts visitors without cookies or tracking, so there's no annoying cookie banner, and you can see how people find you.
- Google gets a fresh list of your pages, which helps it show them in searches.

You'll get a separate email with the details of your new dashboard, where you can do all of this yourself.
