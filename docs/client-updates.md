## Your new dashboard (and a few website improvements)

Hi {name},

We've been busy behind the scenes on your website, and we've built you something new: your own dashboard. It's a simple app that runs on your phone and helps you get more reviews and keep on top of your jobs.

**What's changed on your website**

- Your website looks the same as before. Nothing has moved.
- Your reviews now come from your dashboard, so new ones show up on your site automatically.
- The **"Leave a Review"** button now goes to your own review page, so reviews land straight on your website.
- Opening a photo in your gallery now fills the screen properly on phones, and you can swipe through.
- Visitors can tap **♥** on photos they like. You can choose to show the most-liked ones at the top of your homepage.
- (Roland) The photo viewer had a habit of jumping about when you flicked through photos. That's fixed.
- (Ray) Your project photos are back on your website, at the top of the page and in your gallery.

**Your dashboard**

- **Logging in:** go to **hapitech.dev**, tap **Client login** and enter your email. We'll email you a 6-digit code. There's no password to remember.
- **Put it on your phone:** after logging in, follow the tip at the top of the screen to add it to your home screen. It then opens like any other app.
- **Get a review after a job:** tap **Ask**, then show your customer the QR code. They scan it with their phone camera, tap the stars, and that's it. Not with them? Type their email and we'll send them the link.
- **Reply to reviews:** a quick "thank you" under a review shows customers you care.
- **Jobs:** website enquiries appear here. You can send a quote, book a date (your customer gets a reminder the day before), and send the invoice when you're done.
- **Calendar:** your booked jobs can show in your phone's own calendar.
- **Posts:** share photos of finished jobs. Mentioning the town helps local people find you on Google.
- **My website:** change your service descriptions and photos yourself, any time.
- **Away mode:** going on holiday? Turn it on and your website shows when you're back.

Every page has a **"How does this work?"** tip, and there's a **Help** page with short guides. Or just reply to this email.
