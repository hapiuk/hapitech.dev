## Meet the HapiTech dashboard

Every website I look after now comes with its own dashboard: one place to run the online side of a local business, right from your phone.

- Reviews: ask for one with a QR code or link, reply, and see them all together.
- Jobs: website enquiries arrive here. Quote, book the date, send the invoice.
- Posts: share your latest work on your website and social media in one go.
- Your website: change the words and photos yourself, whenever you like.

It installs on your phone like an app, and you log in with a code by email, with no password to remember. Roland's Handyman and Ray G Handyman Services are the first to get it.

## Every review, every platform, one place

Reviews end up scattered: some on Google, some on Facebook, some sent by text. HapiTech now brings them together.

- Google and Facebook reviews are collected automatically, each one clearly labelled with where it came from.
- Customers can also leave a review directly in about 20 seconds, and are then offered a quick way to post it on Google too.
- Everything shows on the business's own website, and on its page on HapiTech Reviews (hapitech.report).

Good or bad, every genuine review stays up. Businesses can reply, but they can't edit or delete reviews.

## HapiTech Reviews: hapitech.report

HapiTech Reviews is a home for honest reviews of local businesses: every review from every platform, with the business's replies, on one page.

No website? No problem. Your HapiTech Reviews page can be your online home: a link you can share, with your rating front and centre.

Have a look at hapitech.report, or get in touch to add your business.
